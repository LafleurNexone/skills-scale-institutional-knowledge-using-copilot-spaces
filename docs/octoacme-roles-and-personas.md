# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors champion the initiative, maintain its alignment with organizational strategy, and make or secure high-impact decisions and resources.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the initiative's strategic purpose and sponsorship
- Help secure funding, team capacity, and cross-organizational support
- Make or escalate decisions about major scope, priority, and business trade-offs
- Champion the intended outcomes and adoption with leadership

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Project Manager on decision gates, escalations, and resource constraints
- Align with the Product Manager on desired outcomes and priority trade-offs
- Provide direction and decisions to stakeholders while leaving day-to-day delivery coordination to the Project Manager

### Goals
- Keep the initiative aligned with organizational priorities
- Enable timely decisions and remove barriers that exceed the team's authority

### Typical Communication
- Decision-gate and milestone reviews
- Escalations and concise updates from the Project Manager and Product Manager

---

## Engineering / Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering / Delivery Leads coordinate the technical approach and engineering work needed to deliver the agreed product outcomes reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design, engineering capacity planning, and implementation sequencing
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and delivery risks
- Promote maintainable solutions, quality practices, and operational readiness
- Coordinate technical decisions and surface trade-offs early

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Work with the Project Manager to align technical dependencies, estimates, risks, and delivery plans
- Work with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and clarify scope trade-offs
- Guide Developers through design and implementation, and partner with QA/Testing on testability and validation
- Coordinate with Security, Privacy, or Compliance Partners when technical controls or reviews are needed

### Goals
- Deliver a dependable technical solution that meets acceptance criteria
- Make technical risks and decisions visible early enough to act on them

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and engineering planning
- Dependency, risk, and readiness updates to the Project Manager and delivery team

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads translate user needs into usable, accessible experiences and help teams validate design decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and synthesize user needs
- Develop and communicate workflows, interaction designs, and prototypes
- Consider accessibility and usability throughout the design process
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user needs to product goals, priorities, and acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with Developers and the Engineering / Delivery Lead on feasible implementation
- Work with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility considerations in validation
- Share research findings and design decisions with stakeholders

### Goals
- Make the product useful, understandable, and accessible to its intended users
- Reduce avoidable rework by validating important design assumptions early

### Typical Communication
- Research findings, design reviews, prototypes, and usability feedback
- Ongoing collaboration with product and engineering during implementation

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads help teams define measurable outcomes and ensure the data needed to evaluate them is trustworthy and useful.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurement plans and instrumentation needs
- Define or coordinate event tracking, data quality checks, and reporting
- Analyze results and explain limitations or meaningful trends
- Support evidence-based decisions during delivery and after release

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to define success measures and use findings to inform priorities
- Coordinate with Developers and the Engineering / Delivery Lead to implement instrumentation
- Partner with QA/Testing to validate data collection and reporting
- Provide outcome and measurement updates for the Project Manager and stakeholders

### Goals
- Make project outcomes measurable and results understandable
- Help the team make decisions using reliable evidence

### Typical Communication
- Measurement plans, dashboards, data-quality findings, and outcome reviews

---

## Security, Privacy, or Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security, Privacy, or Compliance Partners advise teams on relevant obligations and controls and help plan reviews appropriate to the project's risks.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Advise on risks, controls, review milestones, and evidence needed
- Review proposed approaches and raise gaps early
- Help confirm that required checks and approvals are completed before release

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Work with the Project Manager to capture risks, owners, and review timing in project plans
- Advise the Product Manager on requirements and trade-offs that affect scope or user data
- Collaborate with the Engineering / Delivery Lead and Developers on appropriate technical controls
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on validation and evidence for required checks

### Goals
- Address relevant obligations and risks before they become release blockers
- Make security, privacy, and compliance expectations clear to the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews, design consultations, control guidance, and release-readiness findings

---

## Business / Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Business / Operations Owners prepare affected business processes and operational teams to adopt, support, and sustain the delivered change.

### Responsibilities
- Identify operational impacts, readiness needs, and process changes
- Coordinate training, communications, support arrangements, and handoff
- Define operational acceptance needs, such as support procedures or monitoring
- Gather adoption and operational feedback after launch

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to align business needs, user adoption, and product outcomes
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on launch readiness, dependencies, and handoff plans
- Partner with Developers and the Engineering / Delivery Lead on technical handoff and operational requirements
- Work with QA/Testing to validate operational acceptance and with stakeholders to prepare affected teams

### Goals
- Enable a smooth transition from delivery into sustained operations
- Ensure affected teams can adopt and support the change effectively

### Typical Communication
- Readiness reviews, training and support plans, handoff materials, and post-launch feedback

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Adapt the roles to each project's size and risk; one person may hold multiple roles, but every responsibility should have a clear owner.
